HACKATHON DASHBOARD COMPREHENSIVE UI STUDY

--- 1. CORE DESIGN SYSTEM ---
A. THEME & PALETTE:
   - Primary: #050816 (Deep Midnight Background)
   - Accents:
      * Cyan-400 (#22D3EE): Leadership, primary actions, core branding.
      * Blue-600 (#2563EB): Depth, secondary buttons, gradient fades.
      * Purple-500 (#A855F7): Intellectual/Judging focus, technical stats.
      * Emerald-500 (#10B981): Success, low priority, active status.
   - Typography: Orbitron (Headings), Inter/Sans-serif (Body).

B. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE:
   - GlassCard: Frosted effect with `backdrop-blur-xl`. 1px white/10 border.
   - Gradients: Heavy use of `from-cyan-500/10 to-blue-600/10` for card backgrounds.
   - Animations: Staggered `framer-motion` entrances for all grid items.

--- 2. STUDENT DASHBOARD INTERFACE ---
A. MAIN OVERVIEW:
   - Welcome Beacon: Large card displaying Name, Role, and initial avatar.
   - Mission Telemetry (Stats): 4-card grid showing Team Name, ID, Track, and Size.
   - Team Manifest: Vertical list with "Leader" badges and hover-active member cards.
   - Judging Portal: Double-column layout for Round 1 & Round 2 feedback.
   - Comms Link: Vertical announcement stack with priority-based color coding.

B. TEAM MANAGEMENT:
   - Info Hub: Horizontal summary of team metadata.
   - Member Roster: List with "Remove" capabilities for leaders.
   - Uplink (Invites): Inline email form with real-time validation for adding members.

C. SECURITY (INTERNET PASSWORDS):
   - Hidden-by-default credentials with a password-locked "Reveal" protocol.
   - Copy-to-clipboard functionality for ease of use during the hack.

--- 3. ADMIN CONTROL CENTER ---
A. COMMAND HUB (STATS):
   - High-level telemetry for total Users, Teams, Leaders, and Members.
   - Dynamic refreshing every 60s to ensure live data.

B. OPERATIONS PANELS:
   - Participant Manifest: searchable, filterable list of all registered entities.
   - Team Directory: Detailed view of all registered groups.
   - Announcement Uplink: Form for pushing high-priority updates to all students.
   - Attendance Scanner: Interface for marking physical presence at the venue.

--- 4. AUTHENTICATION PROTOCOLS ---
A. LANDING / LOGIN:
   - Clean, centered interface with floating "Shield" or "Crown" icons.
   - Glassmorphic forms with real-time error feedback in red glass boxes.

B. REGISTRATION (LEADER vs MEMBER):
   - Multi-step forms for Name, Reg No, Email, Phone, Dept, and Password.
   - Leader-specific: Team Name and Track selection.
   - Member-specific: Role explanation box.

--- 5. MOBILE & RESPONSIVENESS ---
- Adaptive Grids: 4-cols (Desktop) -> 2-cols (Tablet) -> 1-col (Phone).
- Navigation: Desktop Navbar -> Mobile Drawer Sidebar with blurred background.
- Typography: Uses `clamp()` logic to ensure readability on small screens.
